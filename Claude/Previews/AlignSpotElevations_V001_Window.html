Align Spot Elevations v1.10.0 — bends to tag, vertical

A new item in the Quick Align to Tag split button lines the leader bend points of the selected spot elevations up in one column with the bend point of a tag you click. It is always vertical, the counterpart of the Horizontal item added in 1.9.0. The leader end and the text follow each bend. Green tags mark what is new.

Sample data: spot labels and positions are made up.

1 · Ribbon, Tagging panel

No new button on the panel itself. The arrow of Quick Align to Tag lists a fourth item. Once you use it, the split button's main click runs it (remembered across Revit restarts).

2 · After the click: status bar prompt

Click the spot elevation to line the bend points up with (Esc cancels)

3 · What happens in the view

Shown for the Horizontal item; the new Vertical item works the same way turned 90°: bend points move left or right onto the clicked tag's column. Pale = before, solid = after. The middle tag is the one clicked (ring). The other bend points move straight up or down onto its row; each leader end and text move with its bend, so the leader shape stays the same. The measured points never move.

What changed in 1.10.0

  • Quick Align Bends to Tag Vertical in the Quick Align to Tag split button: bend points in one column with a clicked tag's bend point, always vertical.
  • The existing Quick Align Bends to Tag still uses the direction last chosen in the window. Neither fixed-direction item changes that saved direction.
  • Spots without a bend point are left as they are. The window and its settings file are unchanged.
