Align Spot Elevations v1.8.0 — distribute buttons

Three new ribbon buttons space the selected spot elevations along their column or row without lining them up: one opens the window in Distribute only, two split buttons do it in one click for the tags, the leader ends or the leader bends. Green tags mark what is new.

Sample data: spot labels and positions are made up.

3 · What happens in the view

Pale = before, solid = after. Even spacing, vertical: the top and bottom tags stay, the three between them get equal gaps. No tag moves left or right.

What changed in 1.8.0

  • Distribute Spot Elev.: opens the window with Distribute only chosen. Even spacing or a fixed gap; text, leader ends and bend points.
  • Quick Distribute Vertical / Horizontal split buttons: one click. Each lists tags (parts last ticked in the window), Ends (leader end points only) and Bends (bend points only), and remembers the item used last.
  • All use the spacing last used in the window. "Keep current spacing" counts as even. Even spacing needs at least three spots that have the part.
  • The window, its settings file and the Quick Align buttons are unchanged.

Earlier

  • 1.7.1: the eight Quick Align buttons became three split buttons that remember the item used last.
  • 1.6.0: Pick tag… on the bend point and end point lines; Quick Align Ends / Bends to Tag.
