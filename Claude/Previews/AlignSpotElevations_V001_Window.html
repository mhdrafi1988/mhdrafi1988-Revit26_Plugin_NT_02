Align Spot Elevations v1.7.1 — align to a tag · quick-align split buttons

What changed in 1.7.1

  • Ribbon only: 8 Quick Align buttons became 3 split buttons by direction. Same commands, same results.
  • Each split button remembers the item you used last until Revit closes.

What changed in 1.6.0

  • Pick tag… on the bend point and end point lines (not the text line). Vertical = same X, Horizontal = same Y; each line remembers its own direction.
  • The clicked tag need not be selected; it never moves, so it may be pinned or grouped.
  • Two quick buttons: Quick Align Ends to Tag, Quick Align Bends to Tag. They use the direction last chosen in the window (default vertical).
