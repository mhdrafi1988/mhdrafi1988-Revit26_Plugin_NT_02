What changes from V029

Preview firstThe preview grows from a 200 px box inside a section to the centre of the window. It draws the sheet, the title block and the usable area, with view names in the boxes.
Three tabs, not eight sectionsLayout holds what you change every time. Spacing holds margins and gaps. Advanced holds tolerances, leftovers and priority groups.
Quick layoutsPack, Rows, Table and Spread set the align and distribute options in one click.
Buttons, not dropdownsAlign and distribute are one-click button rows. Sheet position is a 3 × 3 picker.
Quieter logThe log is one line at the bottom and opens by itself on a warning or error. Counts sit under the preview.
RemovedForce-Fit and Auto-Create Overflow Sheet, which were never implemented. The five metric cards (the counts move under the preview).
FixedClosing the window no longer raises "Revit is currently not within an API context". Also fixed in V029 (29.0.1) and V028 (28.2.1).
Smaller windowOpens at 1180 × 780 instead of a minimum of 1180 × 920, so it fits a laptop screen.
