Revit warning that no longer appears REMOVED

Autodesk Revit 2026 — Warning
An error occurred in family "Concrete-Rectangular Beam" and was automatically resolved, but may require review by the family creator. Error Information: "Can't create sweep" · "Line is too short."
Error Resolution: Delete Element(s)

Before 7.2.1 this came up after an export of a schedule with calculated, combined or room columns.

What changed

  • Cause. To read calculated columns the export tags each element's Comments in a transaction that is always rolled back. Revit rebuilds each element, and a family with faulty geometry raises its warning again.
  • Fix. The warnings are now discarded together with the rolled-back transaction (ScheduleImportEventHandler.ReadScheduleTable).
  • Unchanged. The Excel file, the window layout and import. Export does not change the model, before or after this fix.
  • Still to do in the model. The beams that raised the warning are still faulty. Fix them in the family or on the element.
