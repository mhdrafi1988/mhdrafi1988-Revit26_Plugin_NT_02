Floor Slope tools UPDATED IN 6.32.0

Floor versions of every Roof Slope tool. Floor Slope By Drain V001 and Floor Slope By Point V001 came in 6.31.0. 6.32.0 adds Floor Slope By Point (Ridge) V001, Floor Slope By Point (Multi Copies) V001 and Floor Points & Slope V001 (sections 5–7). Each has the same engine, layout and options as its roof tool. New and floor-specific parts are marked in green. All values are sample data, not a real project. On a narrow screen, scroll each window sideways.

1 · Ribbon: Floor Slope panel, By Point is now a pulldown CHANGED IN 6.32.0

Laid out like Roof Slope: By Drain, a By Point pulldown (By Point, Ridge, Multi Copies) and Points & Slope.
