What changed in 1.2.0

  • Found text area under the preview grid: click a row and every line of that note shows, with its view, line count and character count. Select part of it and press Ctrl+C, or use the buttons.
  • Copy text: the whole current text of the picked note. Copy new text: the whole text after Apply. Copy all found: every note in the preview, separated by a blank line.
  • Copy full text on the right-click menu of each preview row.
  • Each copy is written to the Activity Log. Copying only reads; the model does not change.
