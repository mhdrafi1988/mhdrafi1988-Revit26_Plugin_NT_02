How it behaves

  1. Sheet 1 is the active sheet when you start from a sheet view. Start from any other view and Sheet 1 is empty until you pick one.
  2. The grid lists every placed view on both sheets, grouped by sheet, then by view type. Rows and group headers carry the sheet's colour: blue for Sheet 1, amber for Sheet 2.
  3. The target can be Sheet 1, Sheet 2, any other existing sheet, or a new sheet (number, name, titleblock).
  4. Ticked views from other sheets are taken off their sheet and placed on the target. Ticked views already on the target are re-packed with them. Unticked views on the target stay put and are treated as obstacles.
  5. Layout uses the Sheet Placer packing (margins, gaps, reading order). Views that do not fit go to continuation sheets.
  6. Everything runs in one TransactionGroup, so it is one Undo step and any failure rolls back. Viewports owned by another user (worksharing) are skipped and reported.
  7. Legends stay on their original sheet as well (Revit allows a legend on many sheets); schedules are not moved in V001.
