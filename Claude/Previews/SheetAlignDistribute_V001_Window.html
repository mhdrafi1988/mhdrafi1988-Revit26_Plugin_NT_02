What this tool does — and how it differs from the two tools you already have

It tidies the sheet that is already open, in place. It reads the rows and columns the views are currently sitting in, snaps each row and column to one shared line, and moves nothing further than it has to. No re-ordering, no re-packing, no detail-number sort.

  • Rearrange V030 — re-packs the whole sheet from scratch with a packing algorithm. Views change row, column and neighbour.
  • Arrange Views V001 — re-orders by detail number and rebuilds the sheet as a reading table. Views change position and order.
  • Align & Distribute V001 THIS — keeps every view in its own row and column. The only movement is the few millimetres needed to line things up. A view that would have to travel further than the Max move limit is reported and left exactly where it is.

  • Per-view margin — kept as previewed: pick a view, set its own four-sided clearance, pre-filled from the clear space it currently has, on top of the four sheet margins.
  • Schedules — listed in the grid, unticked by default; tick them per run.
  • Distribute — defaults to Off on both axes, so a run changes nothing but the alignment.
  • Max move — defaults to 25 mm. Raising it from 15 is what prompted the switch from the average to the median for the shared line: with an average, one view 44 mm out of place drags its whole row 15 mm and every view in it trips the guard. With the median the row holds still and only the outlier is refused.
