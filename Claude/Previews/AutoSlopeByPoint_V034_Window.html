Path check spacing (mm)NEW76.2
How far apart the on-roof checks are along each link (default 76.2 mm = V033). Smaller catches narrow openings and thin roof strips but is slower; larger is faster but can let a link cross them. At least 10 checks per link.
Projection tolerance (mm)NEW1
Height margin (default 1 mm = V033) when a point is matched to the roof top face. Raise it if points on sloped or curved roofs are wrongly rejected; too high can accept points just outside the roof.
Exact segment testNEW○ off — Also checks every link against the roof outline and opening edges, so no gap is missed whatever its width. Slower on big roofs.
