Why this order: drafting practice

No published standard ranks annotations in a collision. The US National CAD Standard and office standards cover what to annotate, layers, text sizes and the order of precedence when documents conflict, not which label gives way. Practice agrees on one rule: keep what is tied to geometry, move the most flexible note.

  1. Dimensions are bound to references and dimension strings; moving one changes what it measures or breaks the string.
  2. Room tags identify the space and anchor the finish schedule; they sit inside the room.
  3. Door & window tags link each opening to its schedule (size, type, hardware, fire rating) and must stay next to the opening.
  4. Other element tags usually have a leader and room to move.
  5. Keynote tags replace repeated text with a symbol and legend; they can slide along their leader.
  6. Text notes are the most flexible: free text, often with a leader, so they move first.

Sources: illustrarch: annotating architectural drawings · USITT graphic practice guideline (keynotes) · Allied8 drawing standards: where to key and tag · Ideate Annotate: annotation clash review · Annotation essentials: dimensions, tags, text, keynotes. Your office standard always wins: change the order to match it.

How a resolve works

  • By priority: the lower-priority annotation moves up when it sits above the other, down when it sits below. NEW
  • Never move beats the order: if one side never moves, the other side moves; if both never move, the pair is skipped. NEW
  • Same priority (e.g. two text notes): Distribute, move the upper one up, move the lower one down, or skip the pair. NEW
  • "Up" means up on screen in every 2D view: model height in a section or elevation, screen-up in a rotated plan, up the sheet on a sheet.
  • Distance moved = vertical overlap of the two boxes + the clear gap.
  • Text notes, tags and room tags move as a whole (a tag's leader stays on its host). A dimension moves as a whole where Revit allows it, otherwise only its text.
  • Pinned, grouped, borrowed-by-others and changed-in-central elements are skipped; the Status column says why, and names the element that moved ("Moved 398220"). NEW
  • One run is one transaction: one Undo reverts it. Esc / Cancel during a run moves nothing.
  • Your order, presets, Never move ticks and the same-priority rule are remembered (V006 keeps its own settings file).
