3. What each check reports

Overlap
Two annotation boxes cross. Measured is how far one sits inside the other.
Min distance
They do not touch, but the gap is under the minimum.
Alignment
Elements nearly share an edge or centre line but one is slightly off.
Distribution
A row of three or more with unequal gaps between them.
