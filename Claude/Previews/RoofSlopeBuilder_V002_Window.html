New in V002

  • Draws the ridge lines between drains in the active plan view, as detail lines or as a line-based detail family.
  • If a family can't be placed, that ridge is drawn as a detail line instead, and the report counts it.
  • Marks ridge points (ends, meeting points and points along the ridges) with small detail circles.
  • Find ridges only draws the ridges without changing the roofs.
  • Its own icon, first in the Roof Slope Builder dropdown. Options carry over from V001 on first run.
