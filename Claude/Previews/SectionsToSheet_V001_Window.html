Sections To Sheet: place on an existing sheet

Version 1.2.0. The tool can now target an existing sheet as well as a new one. On an existing sheet it either uses the free space or re-arranges every view on the sheet. Green tags mark what is new or changed. Click the two chips in Target Sheet to switch between the modes.

Sample data: sheet, view and schedule names are made up.

Ribbon tooltip changed

Sections To Sheet (v1.2.0) Lists the section markers shown in the active view (or the selected ones, or picked ones), takes them off the sheet they are on, and lays them out on a new sheet or an existing one, in its free space or re-arranging every view on it: columns that line up, or justified rows, in plan order, bottom-aligned so titles line up. Live preview before anything changes; one Undo step.

What changed in 1.2.0

  • Target Sheet card (was New Sheet): New sheet or Existing sheet, with a sheet picker. The existing sheet's own title block sets the frame.
  • Use free space: everything already on the sheet stays put. The new sections go into the largest empty rectangle inside the margins, using the chosen layout.
  • Re-arrange all views: every viewport on the sheet is laid out again with the new sections, existing views first in their current order. Schedules, text and other items stay where they are and are kept clear of.
  • Views already on the sheet keep their detail numbers; new sections continue from the highest one.
  • Sections that don't fit go on extra new sheets (or are skipped, if Skip and report is on).
  • If the sheet, or a view on it, is owned by another user, the run is blocked or that view is left out, and the reason is shown.
