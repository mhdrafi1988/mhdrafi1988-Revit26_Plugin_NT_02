Void Openings — v0.9.0 (build V001)Finds where MEP passes through structure, sizes each opening from your clearance rules, places a void family with a Job ID and cuts the structure. Works on one structural and one MEP model at a time (the active model or a Revit link). For a linked structural model it writes an opening package to apply inside the structural file.
