What the columns and states mean

  • Status: Detected → Generated (void placed) → Cut (cut verified), or Failed / Rejected / Review.
  • Tracking (re-runs): New, Existing, Updated, Manually modified, Source deleted.
  • Width and Height are for rectangular openings, Diameter for circular ones. All sizes in millimetres.
  • Messages appear in the strip above the status bar, not in pop-up dialogs.
