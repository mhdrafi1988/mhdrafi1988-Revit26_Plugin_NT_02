What each option does

All Off (default) Same layout as V028: the fill strategy's own positions.
Align Rows: Bottom Each row shares one line. Columns stay free.
Rows + Columns: table A column is as wide as its widest view.
Distribute H: Edge to edge First and last view on the area edges, equal gaps between.
Distribute H: Equal spacing Same gap between views and to the edges.

Notes

  • Rows and columns are read from the result of the chosen Row Fill Strategy. Row Tolerance and Column Tolerance decide what counts as the same row or column.
  • Distribute H and Distribute V are greyed out until Align Rows or Align Columns is on.
  • Views already in overflow stay below the sheet and are not aligned.
  • V029 and V028 share one settings file. Saving from V028 turns the new options back to Off.
