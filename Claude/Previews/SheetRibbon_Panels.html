Sheet Ribbon Icons

Plugin 7.41.1 · the Documents tab's Sheet panels, drawn with the real PNG assets from Resources/Icons/SheetTools/.

What changed

7.41.1 — Section Placer had been drawing Sheet Placer's icon since it was added. The two sit one above the other in the Sheet Create stack and both render at 16 px, so they were identical bar their labels; the source even carried a // TODO: swap for a dedicated icon against it. It now has its own: a sheet with two views placed on it, each with its title line. Sheet Placer keeps the icon it had.

On style. SheetTools is split: the older Sheet Create stack (Scope Box, Sheet Placer, Rearrange) uses full‑bleed #2E6E91 tiles, while the newer Sheet Edit and slide‑out icons below use navy line art on transparent. Section Placer takes the tile, because that is what the three buttons in its own stack use and they read as a set. The note at the foot of this page describes the line‑art style, which is what the 7.17.0 icons use.

7.17.0 — Arrange Views had borrowed the Rearrange icon since plugin 2.4.0. Both sit in the same slide-out and both render at 16 px there, so the two buttons were identical apart from their labels. It now has its own. Align & Distribute is new in 7.17.0 and arrives with its own icon.

Sheet Placer and Section Placer, side by side

They sit one above the other in the Sheet Create stack and both render at 16 px, so until 7.41.1 the only thing telling them apart was the label.

Sheet Placer

Places any views on sheets. Keeps the icon it had.

Section Placer NEW ICON

Finds the section views shown in the active plan and packs them onto sheets. The icon shows a sheet with views already placed on it, each with its title line.

The three sheet‑layout tools, side by side

All three move views that are already on a sheet, so their icons have to say which one you are about to run.

Rearrange

Re‑packs the sheet from scratch. Views change row, column and neighbour. Unchanged.

Arrange Views NEW ICON

Re‑orders by detail number into a reading table. The order runs through the views: across the top row, wrapping down, across the bottom.

Align & Distribute NEW

Keeps the order and the grid. Views flush to a guide down the left and along the bottom — the margin corner it aligns them into.

How the icons are drawn

Flat filled shapes on a transparent background: navy outline #1E3A5F, pale blue fill, one bright accent — green #2E7D4F for guides, orange #F08D20 for order. That matches SectionsToSheet and SheetViewsCombine. Note the shared brief at the top of Icon_Prompts.md still describes monochrome line art, which the suite stopped using around plugin 6.5; that file now records the discrepancy.

Each size is drawn on its own at whole‑pixel coordinates with no antialiasing rather than shrinking 32 px to 16 px, because a 1 px outline round a small view turns to mush when it is resampled. At 16 px the views are solid navy instead of outline‑plus‑fill.
