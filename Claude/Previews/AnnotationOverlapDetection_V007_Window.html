How a resolve works with Keep clear ticked NEW

  • Every annotation in the view is an obstacle, including types that are not ticked and pinned ones. Obstacles never move.
  • The distance starts as before: vertical overlap of the two boxes + the clear gap. If the new spot is in the way of anything, the annotation keeps going in the same direction to the first free spot.
  • Neighbour tolerance is the least space kept from every other annotation. With 1 mm, an annotation 0.5 mm away counts as in the way. The clear gap still sets the space to the partner.
  • Maximum move (default 1000 mm, the same view units as the gap: 10 mm on paper at 1:100) caps how far one annotation may travel in a run, counting all its moves. A pair whose free spot is further away is skipped: "no free space within 1000 mm". 0 = no limit. NEW
  • After a pass over the pairs, they are checked again, up to 10 passes, so a later move cannot undo an earlier one. A pair still overlapping after that is skipped: "still overlapping after 10 passes".
  • A pair that is skipped but had already moved in earlier passes is put back: its annotations stay where they were, and Status adds "(its moves were undone)". Any other move that would then land on one of them is taken back too. Moves of pairs that did separate are kept. NEW
  • Before saving, the real boxes are read back. A move that lands on an annotation it did not overlap before is undone, and Status says "Kept in place". The check repeats until it finds nothing, and every good move is kept. NEW Only if a move cannot be put back does the whole run roll back, so a new overlap is never saved.
  • With the box unticked, V007 resolves exactly like V006. Your choice and the tolerance are remembered (V007 settings file; the first time it starts from your V006 settings).
  • One run is one transaction: one Undo reverts it. Esc / Cancel during a run moves nothing.
